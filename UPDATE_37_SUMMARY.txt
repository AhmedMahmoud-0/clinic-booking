MERUNO Update 37 — Phase 5E-1
Family Relationships & Evidence-backed Legal-Guardian Verification

STATUS: Prepared for local application; not yet locally validated.

Migration: 20261108040000_patient_family_relationships_guardian_workflows (seventh migration). It preserves verified legacy guardian rows with an explicit guardianVerificationLegacy marker. No dependency installation, package script change, environment variable, seed, or UI is included. Prisma Client regeneration and one additive PostgreSQL migration are required.

This is Phase 5E-1 only. Family relationship correction/deactivation and user-facing family management remain before Phase 6.
