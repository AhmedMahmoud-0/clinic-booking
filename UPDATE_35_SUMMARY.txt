MERUNO Update 35 — Phase 5C Patient Contacts & Consents

Adds phone/email, address, emergency-contact maintenance and append-only consent/refusal/withdrawal history.
Adds the sixth additive PostgreSQL migration. Existing PatientContact rows remain and default active.

Consent history cannot be updated/deleted in the application or directly in PostgreSQL.
