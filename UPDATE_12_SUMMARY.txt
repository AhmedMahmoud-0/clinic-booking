MERUNO Update 12 — Billing and Database Integrity Hardening

Schema changes:
- DiscountApproval becomes one-to-many from Invoice, keeps history, and adds invoice/requestedAt index.
- AuditLog employee relation changes from ON DELETE SET NULL to ON DELETE RESTRICT.

Migration preview additions:
- Active weekly schedule overlap exclusion.
- One pending discount request per invoice.
- Full payment amount and currency guard, plus deferred invoice/payment/refund state reconciliation.
- Deferred invoice line/subtotal/currency reconciliation.
- Deferred invoice/encounter patient match.
- Radiology schedule/status requirements and resource/procedure modality match.
- Refund invoice ownership, header/item reconciliation, per-item and per-invoice caps, full-payment prerequisite, and invoice locking.

No migration was applied. No database was modified. Seed was not run.
