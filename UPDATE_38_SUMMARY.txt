MERUNO Update 38 — Phase 5E-2: Family Relationship Lifecycle

Adds controlled correction and terminal soft deactivation for directed family relationship records.
- Reception-only PATCH correction endpoint with an enum reason code.
- Reception-only POST deactivation endpoint with an enum reason code.
- Audit events retain relationship changes, actor, bounded reason code, guardian reset metadata, and grant-revocation count only.
- Correcting a verified guardian relationship clears its current evidence-backed guardian state and revokes linked pending/active grants.
- Deactivation retains the relationship row, clears current guardian state, and revokes all linked pending/active grants atomically.
- PostgreSQL prevents reactivation or mutation of a deactivated relationship.
- Delegated access and new delegation issuance now require the underlying relationship to remain active; minor consent signer lookup also checks active relationship state.
- No patient, relationship, grant, document, or audit data is deleted.
