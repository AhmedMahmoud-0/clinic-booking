MERUNO Update 08 — Phase 2B: Appointments and Clinical Records

Schema additions (13 models):
Appointment, AppointmentStatusHistory, QueueEntry, IdentityVerification,
Encounter, ClinicalNote, VitalSign, DiagnosisCatalog, EncounterDiagnosis,
Prescription, PrescriptionItem, ClinicalAmendment, Referral.

Also adds the corresponding relations to Employee, DoctorProfile, DoctorService,
Specialty, and Patient, plus appointment/queue/identity/clinical enums. Appointment references the doctor-specific service using a composite foreign key. Status history can identify an employee or patient actor. Walk-ins are queue entries, not appointments.

No package changes, migrations, seed data, database writes, generated files,
or authentication implementation are included.

Security/design notes:
- Identity verification records evidence types and results, not raw identity values.
- Encounter is separate from Appointment and supports walk-in origin through QueueEntry.
- Appointment price and duration are snapshots.
- Signed note corrections are appended through ClinicalAmendment.
- Authorization remains blocked until Phase 3 is implemented.
- PostgreSQL CHECK, partial unique, and exclusion constraints are documented for Phase 2E.
