MERUNO Update 76 — Patient Records, Billing, Complaints & Notifications

Delivered:
- Protected /portal/records page for completed encounters, issued prescriptions, released lab results, and released radiology reports.
- Protected /portal/billing page for issued invoices, completed payments/receipt records, and refund history.
- Patient complaint submission with strict validation, patient-owned optional appointment/invoice links, server-generated reference, and initial complaint event recorded atomically.
- Patient in-app notifications list and owner-scoped mark-as-read operation.
- Responsive portal navigation links for the new areas.
- Unit validation tests, anonymous-access E2E tests, file guide, apply guide, and implementation notes.

Security:
- Every new endpoint uses the existing verified patient portal authorization guard.
- Patient identity always comes from the server actor, never the client body/query.
- These endpoints expose the signed-in patient's own records only; they do not implement delegated family-record access. No family data is exposed without a later exact-scope `requirePatientRecordAccess()` flow.
- Only released/issued/completed/paid records allowed by the domain lifecycle are shown.
- No raw Prisma objects, patient identifiers, staff notes, or internal complaint events are exposed.

No schema, migration, seed, generated-client, package, lockfile, dependency, environment, or existing domain-workflow changes.

Not included because no approved patient-facing contract existed in inspected archives:
- Patient data-copy/correction request workflow.
- Patient-authorized clinical attachment downloads.
- New notification production or email/SMS/WhatsApp/browser-push delivery.
- Online payment and patient-side refund execution.

Status: delivered for local verification. Update 76 and Phase 10 remain open until local typecheck, lint, unit, E2E, and production-build results are confirmed.
