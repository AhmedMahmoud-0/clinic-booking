MERUNO Update 25 — Phase 3G-B1

Implements a two-address, two-step email change:
- Approval link to the current email (15-minute expiry).
- Verification link to the new email (1-hour expiry).
- SHA-256 token digests stored in the existing Verification table.
- Case-insensitive collision checks across User, Employee.workEmail, and PatientContact EMAIL.
- Atomic serializable update of User.email and linked Employee/Patient contact data.
- Minimal USER_EMAIL_CHANGED audit event.
- Revokes all sessions after success.
- GET page load does not confirm a link; a user click is required.

No dependencies, schema changes, migrations, environment variables, or package scripts.
Adds 10 unit tests and 3 E2E tests. Local validation remains the developer's responsibility.
